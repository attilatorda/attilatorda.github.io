Guard Beacon — Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.guardbeacon.app
Last updated: 1 July 2026

Guard Beacon is an anti-theft “evidence beacon” for Android. If your phone is lost or stolen, it records where the device is and what it can see, and sends that evidence to storage you choose, so you have a trail to help recover it.

The short version. Guard Beacon does not run any servers and has no analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers. The data it captures stays on your device and is uploaded only to the backup destinations you set up yourself (your own Google Drive, WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, or email inbox). The developer never receives, stores, or has access to your data.

1. Who is responsible

Guard Beacon is developed by an independent developer (“we”, “the developer”). For any privacy question you can contact us at codeandcanvaslabs@gmail.com.

3. How the data is used

Captured data is used for one purpose: to build a timeline of evidence that helps you locate or recover the device. It is shown to you inside the app’s event log and written to an evidence file (in NDJSON format) that is uploaded to the destinations you configure.

4. Where the data goes

  • On your device. A copy of each evidence file is always kept in the app’s private storage on the phone, so nothing is lost even if no cloud backup is set up.
  • To backup destinations you choose. If you connect one or more backends, each evidence file is uploaded to them. Supported backends are:
    • Google Drive — using the restricted drive.file scope, which only lets the app see and manage the files it creates. Files go into your own Google Drive.
    • WebDAV (e.g. Nextcloud/ownCloud), S3-compatible storage (e.g. Backblaze B2, Wasabi, AWS S3, MinIO), or Email (SMTP) — all using credentials and destinations that you enter and control.
    These destinations are your accounts. The developer has no access to them and never receives a copy of your data.

You may enable evidence encryption, which locks every upload inside an AES-256 password-protected ZIP using a passphrase only you know. The passphrase is stored on the device in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage and is never transmitted; if you lose it, the encrypted files cannot be recovered.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or transfer it to any third party for their own use. Guard Beacon contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

6. Third-party services

Guard Beacon only contacts third parties as a direct result of features you enable:

  • Google Play services / Google Drive API — used for sign-in and uploads only if you connect Google Drive. Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • api.ipify.org — contacted only if public-IP logging is enabled, to determine the device’s public IP address. See ipify.
  • Your configured WebDAV / S3 / SMTP provider — data is sent to whichever provider you set up; that provider’s own privacy terms apply.

Guard Beacon uses Google’s Drive access within the limits of the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Data retention

Evidence files on the device remain until you delete them or uninstall the app. You can clear the in-app event log at any time from Settings → Data → Delete event log. Copies uploaded to your backup destinations are governed by you — delete them in your Google Drive, WebDAV/S3 storage, or email inbox whenever you wish. Uninstalling the app removes the app’s on-device data.

9. Security

On-device evidence is kept in the app’s private storage. Sensitive settings (such as backend credentials and your encryption passphrase) are stored using Android Keystore-backed encrypted preferences. Optional AES-256 encryption protects uploads in transit and at rest on your backends.

10. Children

Guard Beacon is a general-purpose security tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

11. Your rights and choices

Because your data lives on your device and in destinations you control, you can access, export, or delete it directly at any time — through the app, your file manager, or your cloud/email account. You can also disable any signal, disconnect any backend, or stop protection at will.
