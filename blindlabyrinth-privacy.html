Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 14 August 2026
Developer: Attila Torda

Thank you for choosing to play Blind Labyrinth (“the Game”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles your information.

The short version: Blind Labyrinth collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to. The Android app is not granted permission to use the internet at all. Your settings and your progress stay on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information from you. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Name, email address or phone number
  • Any account or sign‑in details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone
  • Photos, media, or files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers or any other device identifier

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

The Game saves two things locally so that it works the way you left it:

  • Your settings — speech, sound and vibration preferences, and whether level briefings are spoken.
  • Your progress — which levels you have finished, your best time and your best score on each.

On Android this is kept in the app’s own private storage; in the browser build it is kept in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device, and we have no access to it.

If you have Android’s system backup turned on, that small settings file may be copied to your Google account like any other app’s settings, so that a new phone starts where the old one left off. That is between you and Google; the Game never sees it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. “Reset all progress” in Settings erases your progress while keeping your preferences.

3. Third‑Party Services

The Game contains no third‑party SDKs of any kind. There are no adverts, no ad networks, no analytics, and no crash reporting. The Android build does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot contact anything at all.

The browser version loads its own files from the site hosting it, and nothing else — no external scripts, fonts, or trackers. As with any web page, the host serving those files may keep ordinary server logs; that is between you and the host, and it is not something we collect or receive.

4. Permissions

The Android app requests exactly one permission: Vibration. The Game tells you how close you are to a wall by how hard the phone buzzes, so it cannot work without it. It grants no access to any information about you.

5. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any information, we do not use, share, sell, or disclose any of your data — to anyone, for any purpose.

6. Data Security

Because the Game collects nothing and transmits nothing, there is no personal data of yours for us to store, lose, or expose. The data described in section 2 is protected by your device’s own operating‑system protections for app‑private storage.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Data Deletion: We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. The data stored on your device is removed by uninstalling the app, or by using “Reset all progress” in Settings.
  • Backups: You can stop your settings being included in Google’s backup by turning off backup for this app in your device’s system settings.

8. Children’s Privacy

Blind Labyrinth is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included. We comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the protection of children’s online privacy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will post the new policy on this page and update the “Effective Date” at the top.
